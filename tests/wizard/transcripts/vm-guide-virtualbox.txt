How the Workstation VM connects:
  1) Bridged: Bridged networking, to a host network adapter you name next
  2) NAT: NAT networking
     Bridged: the Workstation gets an address on your LAN from the router's DHCP, like any other machine there. Tailscale usually connects directly between machines on the same LAN, which is the fastest path. Recommended.
     NAT: also works; the Workstation only needs outbound internet. Tailscale may relay through its DERP servers when it cannot connect directly, which is slower for Ask, the Library and the Forge.
Number [1]: 1
  Host-only, internal and private networks do not work: the Workstation has no internet there, so its Tailscale sign-in cannot finish.

Tailscale, here and across sites:
  - Deep32 machines talk over your tailnet, so the Workstation, the Forge and the agent PC can be in different places. At home on one LAN the connections are direct. Across sites they still work; the speed depends on whether Tailscale gets a direct path or relays.
  - The Workstation signs in to Tailscale at its first boot. It shows the sign-in address on its console.
  - Publication (d32-publish publish --accept-ct) gives the gateway an HTTPS name on your tailnet. That name appears in public certificate-transparency logs. Only the name: the gateway is still reachable only inside your tailnet.
  - Tailscale access rules (ACLs) must let the agent PC and the Forge reach the Workstation. If your tailnet has custom rules, allow them; the default policy allows everything.

Step 3 of 7: Create the VM
Where: A terminal on the VirtualBox PC
  1)
         VBoxManage createvm --name=Deep32Workstation --register
  2)
         VBoxManage modifyvm Deep32Workstation --firmware=bios --chipset=piix3 --memory=12288 --cpus=6 --graphicscontroller=vboxvga --nic1=bridged --bridge-adapter1="enp3s0" --nic-type1=82540EM
  3)
         VBoxManage storagectl Deep32Workstation --name=SATA --add=sata --controller=IntelAhci
  4) The installer copy on port 0:
         VBoxManage storageattach Deep32Workstation --storagectl=SATA --port=0 --device=0 --type=hdd --medium="/home/you/deep32-downloads/deep32-0.1.0-alpha.2-disc1-workstation-amd64-kvm/installer.vdi"
  5) The empty 200 GB disk on port 1:
         VBoxManage createmedium disk --filename="/home/you/deep32-downloads/deep32-0.1.0-alpha.2-disc1-workstation-amd64-kvm/target.vdi" --size=204800 --format=VDI
         VBoxManage storageattach Deep32Workstation --storagectl=SATA --port=1 --device=0 --type=hdd --medium="/home/you/deep32-downloads/deep32-0.1.0-alpha.2-disc1-workstation-amd64-kvm/target.vdi"

Step 6 of 7: First boot and Tailscale
Where: The VM's window, then this PC
  1) Start Deep32Workstation in VirtualBox.
  2) On its console, set the passwords the first boot asks for.
  3) It then shows a Tailscale sign-in address. Open it on any device and sign in to your tailnet.
  4) If the sign-in stalls: The Workstation checks DNSSEC through the DNS server DHCP gives it. A few routers' DNS breaks that and the sign-in stalls. Then give the VM's network another DNS server.
